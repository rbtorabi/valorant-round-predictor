Holding the defender economy fixed matters. An earlier cut of this table pooled rounds where the defenders were also broke, and appeared to show semi-buy performing as well as a full buy. It does not: controlling for what the other side had, every bracket is a real step up.

Gradient boosting was tried and lost to plain logistic regression on Brier score, so the simpler model shipped — which is why this page runs the real thing rather than a demo. The model is 27 coefficients, about 2 KB; your browser computes a dot product and a sigmoid. Enemy credits stay a manual estimate: survivors carry weapons over for free, so a team’s loadout can outrun its bank, and reconstructing it from round outcomes alone measurably does not work.
